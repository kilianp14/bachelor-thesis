Thus far, however, the opportunities of modeling energy storage systems in such testbeds are severely limited. Either only way too simple battery models with nothing more than an adjustable energy level are supported, or one has to work with too complex and slow simulations of a complete underlying microgrid power network, to study accurate physics-based storage models that consider parameters like battery aging and thermal factors. No work has been done, specifying the interfaces that are necessary to integrate energy storage models of different complexity into a microgrid co-simulation, and discuss challenges that arise when dealing with physics-based battery models in a discrete event simulation.
