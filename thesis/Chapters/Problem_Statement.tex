Thus far, however, such frameworks have only considered simple battery models that have an adjustable energy level, and no approach has been presented that integrate a complex physical battery model into a microgrid co-simulation without simulating a complete model of a power network.

This thesis now aims to investigate, how energy storage models of different complexity can be integrated into a microgrid co-simulation, and discuss challenges that arise when dealing with physics-based models inside a discrete-event simulation.
